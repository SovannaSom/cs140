2. What is the most useful thing you can do with a transform?

The most useful thing we can do with a transform is move, rotate, or resize an element on our webpage

3. What is the difference between a transform and a transition?

A transform changes an element, such as moving, turning, or making it bigger or smaller. A transition makes the change happen slowly and smoothly.
