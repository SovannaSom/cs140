What goes in the header?

Header goes with the name that identifies the webpage.

What goes in the Nav? Can the Nav go in the header? Does it have to go in the header? Does the Nav contain links to another website?

Nav goes as the map that tell reader where to go in the webpage. It can be in the header, before the header, and after the header. Nav can contain to another website but it is encouraged to stay in one website.

What goes in Main?

Main goes with the description and purpose of the webpage.

What goes in the Footer?

Footer goes with legal confirmation, and contact information.
