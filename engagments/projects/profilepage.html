Lola Van

My name is Lola Van and I am a Cambodian Traditional Dancer. I grew up in a province where there was an traditional dance class every weekend. I was very interested in dancing, so I registered for it. After two year, I became a profesional dancer and started my career as a full time dancer. My team and I always performed traditional dance in many events such as wedding ceremmony, water festival, and Khmer New Year.

It was the best experience in my life and it shaped who I am today. I am able to teach and help children in my community to learn Ccambodian traditional dance. This not only contribute to promote traditonal dance, but it makes many children's dreams come true. There are three words that always remember:

  1. Learn
  2. Grow
  3. Serve
