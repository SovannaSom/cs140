Electric Vehicle

The rapid development of technology has brought numerous automobiles to fulfill human needs. During this period of rapid growth, 10 million EVs were sold to consumers around the world in 2020 (Xia & Li, 2022). Electric vehicles are one of the latest developments in transportation technology, focusing on reducing fuel consumption and providing more environmentally friendly transportation. Instead of using gasoline, EVs use batteries to power their motors. Although people consider electric vehicles environmentally friendly, there are concerns about whether EVs are a better choice because of the environmental problems associated with battery production.
